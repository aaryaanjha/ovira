# OVIRA website growth and development rulebook

## Mandatory launch gate

- One clear H1, unique SEO title and description, descriptive URL, and relevant internal links.
- Mobile, keyboard, screen-reader, contrast, focus, reduced-motion, and touch-target checks.
- Working phone, WhatsApp, form, confirmation, 404, sitemap, robots, canonical, and social preview states.
- HTTPS, secure headers, server validation, rate limiting, secrets outside source control, and privacy-safe analytics.
- No sensitive patient data in analytics, pixels, URLs, logs, CRM marketing tags, or browser events.
- Medical content reviewed and approved before publication.
- No guaranteed pregnancy or success claims, fear-based advertising, fake reviews, or unverifiable credentials.
- Core Web Vitals checked on real mobile devices; third-party scripts kept to a minimum.

## Per-page checklist

- [ ] Primary user objective is clear.
- [ ] Direct answer or value proposition appears near the top.
- [ ] Unique SEO metadata and one H1.
- [ ] Logical H2/H3 hierarchy.
- [ ] Relevant internal links and descriptive image alt text.
- [ ] Contextual appointment or enquiry CTA.
- [ ] Accurate, medically responsible content with source and review context.
- [ ] Mobile and keyboard usability verified.
- [ ] Analytics conversion event approved and privacy-safe.
- [ ] Canonical, social preview, sitemap, and indexing state verified.

## Search and discovery

- Use patient intent clusters instead of keyword stuffing.
- Keep location pages useful and genuinely distinct. Do not publish duplicate city pages.
- Use FAQ or medical schema only for accurate visible content.
- Publish original, medically reviewed information and update outdated material.
- Build topical authority and real brand mentions rather than AI-ranking shortcuts.

## Conversion

- Keep forms short and explain what happens after submission.
- Show real clinic availability. Never use countdowns, urgency, or outcome guarantees.
- Measure qualified leads, appointments, attendance, and lead quality.
- Separate marketing attribution from clinical records.
- Obtain and respect applicable communication consent.

## Ongoing operations

- Review Search Console, Core Web Vitals, qualified lead conversion, CRM follow-up, and content accuracy monthly.
- Maintain a medical/compliance review before publication and after material content changes.
- Obtain qualified legal/privacy review for Indian ART and DPDP implementation.
- Use preview deployments, build checks, backups, redirect checks, and rollback procedures.
